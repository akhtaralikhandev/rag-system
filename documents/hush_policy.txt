Hush Withdrawal Policy (Fictional Example)

Creators may request a withdrawal once their available wallet balance reaches £50.

Withdrawal requests are normally processed within 3 to 5 business days.

A creator must have a verified account before requesting a withdrawal.

If suspicious activity is detected, Hush may temporarily hold the withdrawal while the account is reviewed.

Creators can view their withdrawal status from their wallet dashboard.

Hush Support should be contacted if a withdrawal remains pending for more than 5 business days.
